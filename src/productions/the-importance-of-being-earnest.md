## About the Production

Oscar Wilde's masterpiece of wit remains as fresh and funny today as when it premiered in 1895. This beloved comedy follows two friends who create fictional alter egos named "Ernest" to escape their social obligations.

What unfolds is a delightful farce of mistaken identities, outrageous lies, and sharp social satire that skewers Victorian society's obsession with appearances and propriety.
