## About the Production

Tennessee Williams' first major play remains one of the most treasured works in American theater. This haunting "memory play" is narrated by Tom Wingfield, who looks back on his life with his overbearing mother Amanda and painfully shy sister Laura.

Set in a cramped St. Louis apartment during the Depression, the play explores themes of memory, regret, and the tension between responsibility and the desire for freedom.
