## About the Production

A deviously delicious Broadway and Hollywood sci-fi smash musical, *Little Shop of Horrors* has devoured the hearts of theatergoers for over 40 years.

Howard Ashman and Alan Menken's campy rock musical about a man-eating plant has become a household name. Seymour, a meek and downtrodden assistant at a flower shop, stumbles upon a mysterious plant that he names "Audrey II" after his coworker crush. This foul-mouthed, carnivorous plant promises to grant all of Seymour's wishes in exchange for human flesh.
